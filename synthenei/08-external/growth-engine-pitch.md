# Awareness to Revenue — growth engine & 30-day market-pulse POC

Source: Ashique's demo page (HTML) — content preserved here as the editable external doc.

## The idea
Connect prospect research, awareness campaigns, lead capture, ownership, follow-up and commercial outcomes in one operating model, so the team knows what is happening, who owns the next step and which channels create real interest.

## Operating model
| Owner | Responsibility | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Kiruba | Data intelligence & prospect research (advocates, law firms) | Structured, segmented, quality-checked prospect data |
| Venket | Content, email/LinkedIn, Instagram/YouTube short video, Meta Ads | Campaigns and performance learning |
| Gopalakrishnan | UI framework, landing pages, interactive demos | Digital experience and lead-capture surfaces |
| Shaun | RevOps / CRM super admin | Assignment, tasks, process control, funnel & revenue reporting |
| Account Manager | Sales engagement | Timely contact, qualification, demo progression, outcomes |

Flow: Find -> Attract -> Capture -> Assign -> Nurture -> Learn.

## CRM concept
Stages: New, Contacted, Qualified, Demo Scheduled, Demo Completed, Demo Canceled, Converted. Stage (journey position), Status (follow-up condition), Deal outcome (Open/Won/Lost) and Activity status (Not Started/In Progress/Completed/Overdue) are separate fields. Leadership view: leads by source/campaign, positive responses, demo trend (3 months + current), cancellation rate, lead-to-demo and demo-to-won conversion, response time, overdue follow-ups, revenue by source.

## 30-day POC
- Week 1 Foundation: audience, message, prospect data, CRM ownership & tracking, landing page, content approvals
- Week 2 Launch: agreed channels live; validate tracking and lead handling
- Week 3 Observe & engage: reach, replies, engagement quality, enquiry quality; adjust messaging
- Week 4 Review market pulse: compare channels, audience fit, data quality, readiness; recommend next test

Success in month one = a baseline for reach, response rate, engagement quality, enquiry volume, source relevance, contactability and follow-up discipline. Demos or sales may occur but are NOT promised.

## Referral programme (conditional)
Only after legal/compliance review of professional-conduct, solicitation, privacy and platform rules; clear agreements, unique referral IDs, validated conversion, approved payouts.

## Phase two (only with POC evidence)
AI-assisted omnichannel workflow, approved publishing automation (human review, compliant API access), scaling channels that work.

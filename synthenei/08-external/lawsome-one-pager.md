# LAWSOME — Your entire law firm. One clear view.

Legal practice management for Indian law firms.

- **Firm-wide visibility:** cases, hearings, sites and team activity on one dashboard.
- **Case & hearing control:** calendar, tasks, notes, assignments, and flags for missing hearing dates.
- **Multi-site teams:** offices, lawyers, roles and user administration under one organisation.
- **eCourts workflows:** search by CNR, advocate, case number or party name across Supreme, High and District Courts.
- **Ask Nidhi:** an AI assistant that supports legal research — finding relevant internal matters and potentially relevant external judgments for an advocate to review. It does not give legal advice or predict outcomes.

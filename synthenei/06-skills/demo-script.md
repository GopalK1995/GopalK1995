# LAWSOME live demo script (6-8 min + optional AI deep-dive)

Narrative: leadership gets visibility, teams get control, lawyers get case information, AI helps explore legal context.

| # | Segment | Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leadership visibility | 60-75s | Dashboard | Case volumes, status mix, upcoming hearings, growth, sites — one screen |
| 2 | Calendar | 45-60s | Calendar + hearings | Hearings, tasks, notes together; oversight for leaders |
| 3 | Case control | 60-75s | Cases | Filter, see assignee, find a missing hearing date, update/reassign |
| 4 | Organisation | 30-45s | Sites, Users | Offices, roles, who sees what |
| 5 | eCourts | 60s | eCourts | One identifier search, court selection, saved cases, history |
| 6 | Ask Nidhi | 90-120s | Ask Nidhi | New property-dispute matter -> internal matches, external judgments for review, research summary |

Rules: use a matter with verifiable records; show the real response; never imply guaranteed accuracy or outcome prediction. Close by asking for the firm's requirements and booking the consultation.

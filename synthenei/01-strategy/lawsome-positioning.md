# LAWSOME positioning (internal)

Product: LAWSOME — legal practice management for Indian law firms (dashboard, cases, hearings, calendar, multi-site users, eCourts search, Ask Nidhi AI assistant).

## Audience
1. Primary (campaign): law-firm owners, managing partners, senior advocates / practice heads — they buy visibility and control.
2. Secondary: associates, lawyers, legal support — daily users; the route to bottom-up adoption.

## Message
- Headline: **Your entire law firm. One clear view.**
- Buyer thought: "I can finally see what is happening across my firm without chasing every lawyer for updates."
- Three pillars: (1) Firm intelligence (dashboard, sites, statuses, team), (2) Case & hearing control (cases, assignments, calendar, tasks, hearings), (3) AI-assisted research (eCourts + Ask Nidhi).
- Funnel: marketing email -> landing page -> product demo -> book consultation / request access.

## Claims policy (do / don't)
- DO: describe capabilities that exist in the current build; label sample data; say Ask Nidhi "supports legal research".
- DON'T: promise case outcomes, legal advice, guaranteed accuracy, or specific productivity gains unless validated.
- Ask Nidhi demo outputs must be verified against the working build before publishing (internal matches, external judgments with real citations, research summary).

## Compliance watch-list (get legal review before launch)
- Bar Council of India rules restrict advocates advertising/soliciting: we market a software product TO lawyers; the product must not advertise lawyers to the public. Referral/paid-influencer programs need explicit legal review.
- DPDP Act 2023: consent, notice, retention, deletion for lead data; analytics only after consent (implemented).
- eCourts data: confirm terms of use for any scraping/automation; prefer sanctioned access.

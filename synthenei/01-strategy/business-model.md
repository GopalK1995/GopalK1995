# Business model

Goal: win a paying freelance project (first revenue), using a vertical product as proof.

## Legal vertical (primary bet)
Problem: Tamil Nadu lawyers track hearings, case refs, next dates manually.
Solution: system that ingests e-Courts copies/case refs -> case status, next hearing, reminders, client updates.

Go-to-market flywheel:
1. B2C: find individual lawyers (Kiruba data), give a free POC.
2. They use it daily and get hooked.
3. Ask them to champion it to their law firm.
4. B2B: sell firm-wide plan (revenue).

## Other tracks
- Green Pulse: restaurant/DJ venue, digital marketing + strategy (service work, different vertical).
- Marketing platform: shared tool for outbound campaigns (scrape -> segment -> campaign), first used on law firms.
- "Coasters": described as the B2B project; unclear (see open questions).

## Risks / critique
- Scope: three projects + a platform is not one product. Focus = Legal CRM; the rest is later.
- Bar Council of India rules restrict advocates advertising/soliciting. Our campaigns target lawyers as SOFTWARE BUYERS; the product must not market lawyers to the public.
- e-Courts scraping: CAPTCHA, ToS, reliability. Demo must use user-uploaded copies / synthetic data first.
- DPDP Act 2023: client case data is personal data. No real client data in demos or repo.
- Demo data must be synthetic but realistic (Madras HC, district courts, TN case types), clearly labelled.
- No validated buyer yet? A signed/interested firm changes everything.
